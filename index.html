[ 05 / WHY I BUILT IT ]

It started
with a small problem.

I tried several attendance-tracking apps, but many felt unnecessarily complicated, had too many ads, or were slow to use. I wanted something simple, clean and focused on just one thing: tracking attendance without distractions.

So I built Presencia — initially for my own use. What began as a small personal project became an open-source Android app published on F-Droid.

— SHIVANG SAGAR
