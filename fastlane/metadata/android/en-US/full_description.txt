Presencia is a simple, modern and offline attendance tracking app for Android.

Features:

* Add subjects with custom names
* Edit and delete subjects
* Mark class dates as Present or Absent
* View attendance records using a monthly calendar
* View Present, Absent and Total class counts
* Automatic attendance percentage calculation
* Light and Dark themes
* Local device storage
* No account required
* No internet connection required
* No cloud synchronization

All attendance data is stored locally on the device.
